How to play

Batting

Before the ballLeft / right moves you across the crease; up during the run-up steps down the pitch (miss it and you can be stumped)
Arrows / WASDDirection of the shot (down = straight past the bowler, left = off side, right = leg side, up = behind)
SpaceDrive along the ground, just before the ball reaches you (no direction = where the ball wants to go)
E or Shift+SpaceLofted shot (big hit, can be caught)
RRun (press again for another)
BBefore the ball reaches you: defensive block. During a run: go back (first half of a run)

Bowling

CChange the bowler (before the first ball of an over)
Q / EChange the delivery (swing, yorker, bouncer, slower, spin variations)
Arrows / WASDMove the yellow marker: where the ball pitches
SpaceStart the run-up
Space againStop the meter in the green zone for a perfect ball; red risks a no-ball

Rules

OutBowled, caught (including edges to the keeper and slips), run out and stumped. No LBW in this version.
ExtrasWides and no-balls give a run and are bowled again. A no-ball gives a free hit.
MatchBoth teams bat for the chosen overs (10 wickets). Highest score wins.
ExitHold the × button at the top (or hold Esc) to close the game and free its memory. Main menu in the pause menu goes back to the menu.
